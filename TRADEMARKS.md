## Ownership and designation

Release materials use **TWTTY™** at prominent branding mentions. The designation
indicates an unregistered trademark claim, not a registered trademark.
Commands, file paths and repository identifiers remain unchanged.

Trademark ownership and the rights holder's basis to claim the mark have not been
independently verified by this release-content review. Adding the designation
does not establish ownership or legal clearance for publication. A
registered-trademark symbol must not be used without the applicable granted
registration.

Trademark registration is a separate legal process, not part of release
automation. No application is being submitted by preparing this repository.
